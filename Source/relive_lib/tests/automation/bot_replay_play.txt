# The bot plays the start of Mines path 1 by the "any" profile (down two hoists, a roll under a low ceiling, a hand
# stone, a running jump to a ledge, up to the first Mudokon) and is killed on the way (electrocute): it has
# to notice, go back to a checkpoint and carry on. The buttons of the frames that counted are saved, and
# bot_replay_replay.txt plays them back with no bot and no death: both must end in the same world
# on every frame they share (see run_bot_replay.cmake), which shows the rewind is exact and the run that counts
# is death free.
timeout 200
start mines 1 4 838 1498
draw 0
wait_until in_game
wait_frames 40
hash_log %OUT%
bot any,seed=3
wait_frames 100
electrocute
wait_until camera mines 1 6
bot_inputs %INPUTS%
bot_stop
expect_bot_restores 1
wait_frames 4
wait_frames 14
quit
